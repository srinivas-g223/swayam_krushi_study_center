Smart Study Tips

Simple habits that help every competitive exam aspirant

⏰

Follow a daily timetable

Fix time for each subject and stick to it, even on busy days.

📰

Read the news daily

Current affairs carry marks in almost every exam. Make notes every day.

📝

Solve previous papers

Previous question papers show the pattern and the important topics.

✅

Take regular mock tests

Timed tests build speed and accuracy before the real exam.

🔁

Revise every week

Short weekly revision helps you remember what you have studied.

💪

Stay healthy and consistent

Sleep well and keep going. Steady effort beats last-minute rush.
